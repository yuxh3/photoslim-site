隐私政策

净相册 PhotoSlim · 更新日期：2026 年 11 月 14 日

一、我们收集什么

我们不收集你的照片、视频或任何个人身份信息。App 不需要注册账号，不接入统计、广告或第三方分析 SDK。所有照片分析（去重、质量判断、截图识别）都在你的设备上完成，不会上传到任何服务器。

二、相册权限

App 申请相册访问权限，仅用于读取照片与视频并在本机进行分析、分组和清理。拒绝授权时无法使用清理功能，但你可以随时在系统设置中重新授权。

三、删除行为

任何删除都需要你在 App 内确认，并再次经过系统弹窗确认。删除的照片会进入系统「最近删除」，通常保留 30 天。请注意：如果你开启了 iCloud 照片，删除会同步到同一账号下的其他设备。

四、本地数据

App 仅在本机保存外观、语言等偏好设置，不包含照片内容。卸载 App 后这些设置会被清除。

五、第三方服务

当前版本不包含广告、统计或数据共享服务。若未来引入，我们会在更新说明与本政策中明确告知。

六、儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会主动收集儿童信息。

七、你的权利

由于我们不收集个人数据，不存在需要导出或删除的账户数据。你可以随时在系统设置中撤销相册权限。

八、联系我们

九、政策更新

政策发生重大变更时，我们会在 App 内公示并更新本页面的日期。

Privacy Policy

PhotoSlim · Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. What we collect

We do not collect your photos, videos, or any personal data. The app requires no account and includes no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs. All analysis runs on your device and nothing is uploaded.

2. Photo library access

The app requests photo library access solely to read photos and videos for on-device analysis, grouping, and cleanup. You can revoke access at any time in Settings.

3. Deletion

Every deletion requires your confirmation in the app and a second confirmation from iOS. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted and are typically kept for 30 days. If iCloud Photos is enabled, deletions sync to your other devices.

4. Local data

Only preferences such as appearance and language are stored locally. No photo content is stored. Uninstalling the app removes these preferences.

5. Third parties

No advertising, analytics, or data-sharing services are included. If this changes, we will disclose it in the release notes and this policy.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

7. Your rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is no account data to export or delete. You may revoke photo access in system settings at any time.

8. Contact

Questions? Email 18879217908@163.com.

9. Changes

Material changes will be announced in the app and reflected in the date above.
