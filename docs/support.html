常见问题

照片是真的被删除了吗？

是。删除会经过 App 内确认和系统二次确认，然后从相册移除并进入系统「最近删除」，30 天内可恢复。开启 iCloud 照片时，删除会同步到其他设备。

为什么提示"免费额度已用完"？

免费版每天可以完成 1 次清理，单次最多 100 张。Pro 版不限次数和数量。

删除后能撤回吗？

App 内的「撤销」只对还没结算的待清理队列有效。已经删除的照片请在系统「最近删除」中恢复。

视频为什么打不开？

如果视频存在 iCloud 上，首次播放需要先下载，可能需要几秒；网络不佳时会失败，请稍后重试。

会收集我的照片吗？

不会。所有分析都在设备本地完成，照片不会上传。详见隐私政策。

FAQ

Are photos really deleted?

Yes. Deletion requires your confirmation in the app and a second confirmation from iOS, then items move to Recently Deleted and can be restored within 30 days. With iCloud Photos enabled, deletions sync to your other devices.

Why do I see "Free limit reached"?

The free plan allows one cleanup per day, up to 100 photos. Pro is unlimited.

Can I undo a deletion?

The in-app undo only applies to items still in the pending queue. Deleted photos can be restored from Recently Deleted in Photos.

Why won't a video play?

Videos stored in iCloud need to download first, which may take a few seconds. Retry on a better connection if it fails.

Do you collect my photos?

No. All analysis runs on your device. See our Privacy Policy.
